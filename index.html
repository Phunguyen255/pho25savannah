About us

Welcome to Pho 25, where authentic Vietnamese flavors meet warm Southern hospitality. Every bowl of pho is crafted with slow-simmered broth, fresh herbs, premium ingredients, and recipes inspired by Vietnam's rich culinary traditions. Whether you're joining us for a comforting meal, a quick lunch, or dinner with family and friends, we're dedicated to serving fresh, flavorful dishes that make every visit memorable.

Our signature pho starts with a slow-simmered broth, complemented by premium meats, fresh herbs, and perfectly cooked noodles. Beyond pho, our menu features flavorful rice plates, vermicelli bowls, appetizers, and traditional Vietnamese beverages—all prepared with care and served with genuine hospitality.
